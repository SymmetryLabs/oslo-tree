# Credits

Everything in the scene is generated by this repository's code (MIT OR
Apache-2.0): the tree from its show package, the leaves, trunk and light by
`li-viz`, and the harbour, water, grass, stone and city lights by the site's
own geometry and shaders (`apps/oslo-site/src`). No photographs and no
image textures are used, so there are no texture credits; any CC0 texture
added later is listed here with its source.

The recorded views (`media/`) are renders of the same scene by the same
code, made by `tools/build-oslo-site.sh`.
